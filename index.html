Common questions

Answers to what shop owners usually ask before getting started.

Which areas does NOTEORA deliver to?

Kandy and surrounding areas, the Kurunegala area, and shops along the Kurunegala–Mahiyanganaya Road.

Do you provide daily delivery?

Yes — NOTEORA operates a daily delivery service to keep shop stock levels consistent.

Do you deliver directly to shops?

Yes, deliveries go straight to your shop or business premises.

Which dairy products are available?

Contact us with what your shop sells, and we'll confirm what's available for delivery to your area.

What time are deliveries made?

Deliveries run on a daily schedule agreed with each shop. Get in touch to confirm timing for your location.

Can I place a regular order?

Yes — most shops on our route work with us on a regular, ongoing order rather than a one-off delivery.

What is the minimum order quantity?

This depends on the products and your shop's needs. Contact NOTEORA to discuss quantities.

Can new shops register for delivery?

Yes. Reach out with your shop's location and we'll confirm whether you fall along the current delivery route.

How can I contact NOTEORA?

By WhatsApp, phone, or email — details are below in the contact section.
